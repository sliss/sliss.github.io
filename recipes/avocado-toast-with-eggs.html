Avocado Toast with Eggs

Breakfast, Quick

Prep Time: 3 minutes Cook Time: 5 minutes Servings: 1 serving Source: Steven Liss (original)

Ingredients

1 slice sourdough bread

1/2 avocado

2 large eggs

Nutritional yeast, to taste

Everything bagel seasoning, to taste

Description

A go-to breakfast: sourdough, avocado, and two eggs, finished with nutritional yeast and everything bagel seasoning.

Directions

Toast the sourdough.

Meanwhile, cook the eggs to your liking (fried, poached, or scrambled).

Mash the avocado onto the toast.

Top with the eggs, then sprinkle with nutritional yeast and everything bagel seasoning.
